### P019 — Reconfiguration of optimum no-three-in-line configurations

**Classical model:** a subset of $\{0,\ldots,n-1\}^2$ with no three collinear, of **maximum possible cardinality** $m(n)$. Let $\tau(n)$ be the least number of simultaneously replaced points sufficient to connect all maximum configurations under valid exchanges (preserving maximum size and general position at every intermediate state).

**Proposed structural question:** is $\sup_n\tau(n)<\infty$? Otherwise determine the asymptotic growth and obstructions.

**What is proved:** if $m(n)=2n$, every row and column has exactly two selected points. A one-point replacement would have to preserve both its row and its column and is therefore impossible. Every nontrivial two-point move has the form of a rectangle switch between two rows and two columns; collinearity can forbid even these.

**Exact finite results (not a general theorem):**

| $n$ | Solutions of size $2n$ | Components under $\le2$ replacement | $\tau(n)$ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| 4 | 11 | 7 | 4 |
| 5 | 32 | 12 | 3 |
| 6 | 50 | 46 | 5 |
| 7 | 132 | 92 | 6 |

The two-swap-universally-connected hypothesis is **false already at $n=4$**. The surviving open question is the **minimum move size for universal connectivity**, not the naive failed conjecture. Precise reproducible program: [P019 exact solver](../experiments/P019_no_three_line_exchange.py).

**Closest sources and exact overlap:**
- [Guy–Kelly (1968), *The No-Three-In-Line Problem*](https://doi.org/10.4153/CMB-1968-062-3): the underlying extremal configurations and early counts are classical.
- [Prellberg (2026), *Constraint satisfaction programming for the no-three-in-line problem*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2026.106244): optimized construction search, not the $\tau(n)$ exchange statistic in its abstract.
- [Flammenkamp (updated October 2026), no-three-in-line database](https://wwwhomes.uni-bielefeld.de/achim/no3in/readme.html): constructions, symmetries and counts.
- [*The extensible No-Three-In-Line problem* (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejc.2023.103796): dense infinite-grid general-position sets, not bounded exchange connectivity between optimum finite grids.

**Novelty status:** targeted search failed to find the *precise* optimal-solution exchange-connectivity invariant, but geometric reconfiguration is broad and equivalence may be hidden in earlier work. **Not certified new or unresolved.**

### P020 — $p$-adic Hankel cokernel distribution

**Definition:** $H_n=(X_{i+j})_{0\le i,j<n}$, with independent Haar-uniform $X_i\in\mathbb Z_p$. Its cokernel $C_n=\mathrm{coker}H_n$ is a finite abelian $p$-group almost surely. The **new question proposed** is whether the distribution converges for fixed $p$ on isomorphism classes of finite abelian $p$-groups, and if so whether the limiting law has a useful formula.

**Crucial known result, not new:** the classical rank enumeration for uniform finite-field Hankel matrices gives for $1\le k\le n$

$$
\Pr(\mathrm{corank}(H_n\bmod p)\ge k)=p^{-(2k-1)}.
$$

In particular, $\Pr(C_n=0)=1-p^{-1}$ for **every** $n$, not the independent-entry Cohen–Lenstra limit $\prod_{j\ge1}(1-p^{-j})$. This is an **exact obstruction to universality**; do not misstate our open question as whether it is Cohen–Lenstra. Rank modulo $p$ measures the number of group generators, not the higher invariant factors, so the exact full **$p$-adic group law** is a distinct statistic.

Finite field rank counts by exhaustive enumeration agree with the classical formula: $p=2$ at $n=2,3,4$ and $p=3$ at $n=2,3$ (program [P020 exact screening](../experiments/P020_hankel_finite_field_ranks.py)).

**Closest sources:**
- [Dwivedi–Grinberg, *On the rank of Hankel matrices over finite fields* (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2022.02.014): **mandatory priority reference** for mod-$p$ rank laws.
- [Wood, *Random integral matrices and the Cohen–Lenstra heuristics* (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1353/ajm.2019.0008): independent-entry random cokernel law, a **different model**.
- [Maples, *Cokernels of random matrices satisfy the Cohen–Lenstra heuristics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.1239): further independent-entry predecessor.
- [Jang–Kaplan–Lee–Yu, *A mod $p$ determinant criterion for Cohen–Lenstra convergence with prescribed zero patterns* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06993): support-pattern dependence, generally **independently random nonzero entries**, rather than Hankel repeated variables.
- [Miller–Stanton, *Orthogonal polynomials and Smith normal form* (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.03539): structured **deterministic** Hankel Smith forms.

**Novelty status:** no exact prior *random Haar-Hankel $p$-group distribution* located in targeted accessible search results; possible unexamined links to random linear recurrence, rational functions, moment matrices and $p$-adic structured Smith forms. **Originality not certified.**
