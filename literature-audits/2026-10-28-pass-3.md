## P007 — Maximum distinct differences on a coprime Hamiltonian path

Closely related established objects:

- [Graceful Labeling — MathWorld](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/GracefulLabeling.html), including path permutations with all differences \(1,\ldots,n-1\).
- [Francesco Monopoli, *Absolute Differences Along Hamiltonian Paths* (2015)](https://doi.org/10.37236/5159), about distinct differences in complete graphs and arbitrary arithmetic-progression labels.
- [Rohan Cattell, *Graceful labellings of paths* (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2007.03.046).
- [Wolfram MathWorld, *Coprime Graph*](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/CoprimeGraph.html).
- [M. H. A. Bani Mostafa and E. Ghorbani, *Hamiltonicity of a coprime graph* (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00373-021-02362-1).
- [An extensive survey of graph labelings](https://www.combinatorics.org/files/Surveys/ds6/ds6v24-2021.pdf), including *prime labeling* (consecutive vertices receive coprime labels).

The simple full-gracefulness variant is **not open**: the parity argument in P007 proves it is impossible for \(n\ge6\). The remaining P007 question is about attaining the exact maximum *number of distinct* differences. Targeted searches using “coprime graceful permutation,” “coprime Hamiltonian distinct differences,” and “relatively prime graceful path” revealed no explicit formula for this parameter, but the lack of a hit is **not** a novelty certificate.

## P008 — Percolation on the arithmetic coprime host

The **model itself is not new**:

- [Xiang Qi, *Random Meta-Structure: A Random Graph Model Based on Abstract Binary Relations* (2026 preprint)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21737868), Section 5.3, explicitly takes \(R(i,j)\iff\gcd(i,j)=1\), independently retains edges, and discusses *heuristic giant-component emergence*; its general isolated-vertex expectation formula is compatible with ours.
- [Bollobás–Borgs–Chayes–Riordan, *Percolation on dense graph sequences* (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1214/09-AOP478), develops threshold phenomena for percolated dense host sequences.
- [Chung–Horn–Lu, *Percolation in General Graphs*](https://cs.du.edu/~paulhorn/pap/subgraph-j-final.pdf), studies general host criteria for giant components.
- [Erdős–Sárközy, *On cycles in the coprime graph of integers* (1997)](https://doi.org/10.37236/1323), for structure of the deterministic host graph.
- [Coprime Graph — MathWorld](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/CoprimeGraph.html).

We derived a **leading-order sharp connectivity threshold**, a **Poisson isolated-vertex law** and a **Gumbel connectivity window at an implicit arithmetic center**. These proofs are written in P008, not claimed original relative to every existing theorem. An external expert should check whether general theorems already imply the result, and audit all quantifiers and rates.

The live candidate question is **explicit asymptotic centering of the window to precision** \(o(\log\log n/n)\). This is distinct from generic giant-component emergence and from merely locating the leading-order connectivity threshold.

## Rejected/question resolved during triage

“Can one order \(\{1,\ldots,n\}\) with adjacent entries coprime and with all absolute consecutive differences distinct?” is **fully resolved**, with an elementary iff answer \(n\le5\). It is explicitly recorded in P007 rather than promoted as an independent open problem.
