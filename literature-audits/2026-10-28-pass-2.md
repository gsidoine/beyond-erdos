# Literature audit — discovery pass 2 (2026-10-28)

**Scope:** targeted public-web searches, not an exhaustive mathematical bibliography review. **Outcome:** P005 and P006 remain *candidates*, not verified new open problems.

## P005 — coprime-permutation switches

- [Carl Pomerance, *Coprime permutations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03085): existence and counting of permutations $\sigma$ with $\gcd(i,\sigma(i))=1$.
- [Sah–Sawhney, *Enumerating coprime permutations*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.06268): asymptotic enumeration.
- [OEIS A005326](https://oeis.org/A005326): exact total counts used to independently check the exhaustive component computation.
- [The Perfect Matching Reconfiguration Problem](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2019.80): general alternating-four-cycle reconfiguration is a developed, sometimes hard subject.
- [Counting Perfect Matchings and the Switch Chain](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/137388/7/Dyer%20NonbipartiteSIDMA-final.pdf): connectivity criteria and counterexamples in general graphs.

**Overlap assessment:** Coprime permutations and four-cycle reconfiguration are both known objects. I did not locate a paper giving a connectivity theorem or counterexample specifically for the bipartite $\gcd(i,j)=1$ graph. A non-hit does not establish novelty, and the possibility of a general theorem settling the special instance remains open to audit.

## P006 — prime-difference-free Sidon sets

- [Sidon sets and Golomb rulers](https://mathworld.wolfram.com/SidonSet.html): classical $(1+o(1))\sqrt N$ extremal cardinality.
- [Carter–Hunter–O'Bryant, *On the diameter of finite Sidon sets* (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10474-024-01499-8): modern upper bounds.
- [Erdős #42](https://www.erdosproblems.com/42): difference avoidance between Sidon sets, not identical to exclusion of all prime differences.
- [Croot et al., *A combinatorial large sieve for Sidon sets, distances, and norm forms* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17487): related sieve tools, not claimed to answer P006.

**Overlap assessment:** Forbidding a prescribed set of differences is a classical additive-combinatorics theme, so an unrecognized equivalent question is possible. Targeted searches on "Sidon set prime differences", "Golomb ruler avoiding prime differences", and "Sidon sets all differences composite" did not reveal a direct resolution. Broader historical checking is required.
