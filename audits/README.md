# Mathematical audits

These dated research records contain proof examinations, counterexamples, computational checks, and comparisons with published work. Each document records its stated scope and date. Subsequent results may supersede its conclusions.

The [current catalogue](../README.md#problem-catalogue) and individual dossiers give the current status of the 46 numbered entries. The [current curation](../data/curation.json) records their collection assignments.

The [P001–P041 source comparison](literature-comparison-P001-P041.md) identifies the nearest established literature for every numbered entry and the outstanding priority distinctions. The [P001–P035 consolidation](consolidation-P001-P035-novelty-and-proof-gates.md), [P001–P030 portfolio](portfolio-consolidation-P001-P030.md), [P001–P025 literature comparison](2026-10-29-P001-P025-literature-source-gate.md), and [P001–P020 review](2026-10-29-P001-P020-first-collection-consolidation.md) are historical assessments of the indicated sets of problems. Earlier conclusions about manuscript status, priority, or unresolved cases are not necessarily current.

A [repository maintenance record](repository-integrity-and-branch-review.md) documents the disposition of obsolete branches. Mathematical evidence is contained in the dossiers, theorem notes and associated computations.
