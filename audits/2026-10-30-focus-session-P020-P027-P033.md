# Focus-session research audit — P020, P027, P033

**Date:** 30 October 2026. **Status:** exploratory and adversarial; no new conjecture ID. This note distinguishes a genuine proved boundary result from attractive but unfinished extensions.

## P020: second-order all-exponent-two law, obstruction and boundary

The earlier P020 conditional restriction theorem proves every elementary-abelian group law; PR #45 extends to second-order residual nullities one and two. A natural attempt to extend the probabilities of `(Z/p)^(k-j) + (Z/p²)^j` to *all* j meets a specific obstruction. Free third digits generate the full **linear Hankel subspace** on a polynomial-shift radical of dimension j, but the nonlinear Schur-complement's fixed offset need not itself be Hankel. An affine translate of the Hankel subspace is not known to preserve its invertible fraction. Hence **the proposed j>=3 general formula is not proved**, and the original three-generator `(p²,p²,p²)` probability for n>3 remains unresolved.

An **exact boundary law** does follow directly when n=k. The cokernel is `(Z/p²)^n` iff all of the original `2n-1` independent Hankel coefficients vanish modulo `p²` and the scaled matrix is nonsingular modulo p. By the classical finite-field Hankel nonsingularity count,

$$
\Pr(C_n\simeq(\mathbb Z/p^2)^n)
=p^{-2(2n-1)}(1-p^{-1}).
$$

At p=2,n=3 this is `1/2048`; 16 of the 32 binary 3×3 Hankel matrices are invertible. This identity is elementary once the rank formula is known and is **not promoted as independent novelty**. See [the precise obstruction note](P020-second-order-full-radical-obstruction.md).

**Next decisive research test:** classify the fixed second-order affine offset modulo the linear Hankel subspace for the smallest nontrivial case `k=j=3<n`. A counterexample to Hankel invariance is valuable even without a new closed-form probability.

## P027: exact bottleneck versus a genuinely new mixing lower bound

The existing [finite arithmetic-template theorem](../theorems/P027-finite-arithmetic-template-transfer.md) proves for each **fixed** T that c_T n+O_T(1) independent copies of the finite heat bath appear in the full chain. Their gap λ_T gives

$$
\liminf_{n\to\infty}
\frac{t_{\mathrm{mix}}(n,\varepsilon)}{n\log n}
\ge \frac1{2\lambda_T}.
$$

Taking the supremum over **fixed** T is legitimate. Letting T grow with n within the finite-copy proof is **not justified**: the positive density constant c_T and O_T(1) remainder depend on T. This quantifier order is a crucial barrier to any claimed super-n log n result.

The existing finite values of λ_T are nonmonotone. The attempt to derive `inf_T λ_T=0` from the isolated 23-state factor yields no proof: an embedded slow coordinate can force a lower bound but cannot by itself force the *sequence of local gaps* toward zero.

**Next decisive research test:** construct T_m and explicit unit-variance functions f_m on Ω_Tm with Dirichlet quotient tending to zero; alternatively prove a uniform Poincaré inequality across these finite distributive-lattice state spaces. Neither direction follows from the present lower-bound formula.

## P033: prime palette count and the analytic tail

The [density theorem](../theorems/P033-prime-palette-density-and-reflection-criterion.md) gives an asymptotic positive fraction of coprime half-difference triples, using PNT in fixed arithmetic progressions plus a Brun–Titchmarsh estimate. The finite calculation up to 10^6 is separate.

The exact comparison `connected iff gcd((q-p)/2,(r-p)/2)=1` applies only under the **near-n interval hypotheses** of the deterministic reflection criterion; it is not a universal equivalence for arbitrary prime palettes. The derived prime visibility Euler product uses familiar prime equidistribution machinery and does not establish a new short-prime-gap theorem. There remains **no explicit analytic threshold** overlapping the million-case computation, hence the all-n question remains open in this project.

A tempting inference from many short cycles to expansion, Hamiltonicity or two-vertex connectivity is invalid: abundant vertex-disjoint six-cycles do not force a global spanning property. P034 therefore remains independent and provisional.

**Next decisive research test:** obtain an explicitly numerical prime-in-progressions/sieve threshold compatible with the deterministic reflection criterion, or find a direct construction not depending on an asymptotic density argument.

## Research quality gate

No claim of external refereeing or exhaustive priority verification is made. In particular, the P020 higher-nullity formula and the P027 vanishing-gap conjecture must not be represented as new theorems. Recording failed routes protects the next focus session from repeating them.
